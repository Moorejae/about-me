How I Help

Expertise

1 - AI Workflows & Automation

I design workflows where AI does the repetition and a deterministic layer holds the line. Spec-driven prompts, multi-step automation with human checkpoints, regex and schema gates before any real-world action, and MCP servers, APIs and webhooks wired into the tools a team already uses. Models: Gemini, Claude and GPT, with Vertex AI where a client needs the work inside their own Google Cloud project.

2 - QA, Evaluation & Guardrails

Testing is the part that makes the rest trustworthy. I write the checks, reproduce bugs from logs and tests, log the edge cases a model gets wrong, and compare models against each other instead of trusting a benchmark table. On REZMO that meant 2,000+ backend tests and 480+ frontend tests; on the forecast model, 145 tests written from the spec plus an audit of every settled result.

3 - Support, Onboarding & Delivery

Nine years of face-to-face sales, support and de-escalation, then support queues and Jira triage day to day. I handle ticket queues, SLAs and reproduction, write the onboarding guides and troubleshooting docs that let a user finish the job without opening a ticket, and gather requirements from non-technical stakeholders. Rollout ends in a handover rather than a dependency.

4 - Cloud Infrastructure & Technical Fluency

Cloud infrastructure on Cloudflare Workers, Pages, DNS and WAF, plus AWS, Google Cloud and Oracle Cloud, with origin lockdown, TLS and self-hosted mail on the REZMO domain. Alongside that: APIs, webhooks, MCP and OAuth 2.0, and SQL and PostgreSQL for investigation. I can read a log, test an endpoint, reproduce a bug and write a report an engineer can act on, which is the difference between escalating and actually solving.

5 - Website Design, Build & Deployment

I design and build websites: layout and visual systems, motion, responsive front-ends and the hosting underneath them, Cloudflare Pages and Workers, Netlify, Hugging Face, or a hardened VPS with Nginx. That includes DNS, TLS, WAF rules and self-hosted email on the same domain, so a client gets a site that is reachable, not just a design file. BEM Africa and Mind Renewal Care are the two builds on this site, both previews so far.

6 - Triage, Escalation & Documentation

I can run the desk as well as the build. Jira for triage, plus ticket queues, SLAs and customer history on the support desk, reproducing bugs, writing the fix, or escalating with a report an engineer can act on. Nine years of customer work is why de-escalation is a default rather than a script, and why the products I build are written to fail loudly and say what went wrong.
