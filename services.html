How I Help

Expertise

1 - Customer Support, Onboarding & Docs

Nine years of face-to-face sales, support and de-escalation before tech, then Zendesk and Jira day to day. I handle ticket queues, SLAs, triage and reproduction, and I write the onboarding guides and troubleshooting docs that let a user finish the job without opening a ticket. OAuth and API problems get explained in plain English, not jargon.

3 - Product & Client Delivery

Requirements gathering with non-technical stakeholders, QA testing and edge-case logging, clear status updates, and an end-to-end rollout that ends in a handover rather than a dependency. This is the part of the work I care most about: the client should be able to run the thing after I leave.

2 - AI Workflows, Guardrails & Evals

I design workflows where AI does the repetition and a deterministic layer holds the line. Spec-driven prompts, multi-step automation with human checkpoints, regex and schema gates before any real-world action, and model benchmarking that compares output instead of trusting it. Models: Gemini, Claude and GPT, with Vertex AI where a client needs the work inside their own Google Cloud project.

4 - Technical Fluency

APIs, webhooks, MCP and OAuth 2.0. SQL and PostgreSQL for investigation, Cloudflare Workers, Pages, DNS and WAF, plus AWS and Google Cloud environments. I can read a log, test an endpoint, reproduce a bug and write a report an engineer can act on, which is the difference between escalating and actually solving.

5 - Website Design, Build & Deployment

I design, build and ship websites: layout and visual systems, motion, responsive front-ends and the hosting underneath them, Cloudflare Pages and Workers, Netlify, Hugging Face, or a hardened VPS with Nginx. That includes DNS, TLS, WAF rules and self-hosted email on the same domain, so a client gets a site that is live and reachable, not just a design file. BEM Africa and Mind Renewal Care are the two builds on this site.

6 - Triage, Escalation & Documentation

I can run the desk as well as the build. Jira for triage and Zendesk for queues, SLAs and customer history, plus reproducing bugs, writing the fix, or escalating with a report an engineer can act on. Nine years of customer work is why de-escalation is a default rather than a script, and why the products I build are written to fail loudly and say what went wrong.
