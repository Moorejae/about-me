Mind Renewal Care

A therapy NGO site where the audience arrives anxious, often on a phone. Clarity first, then craft.

ClientMind Renewal Care
RoleDesign, build, mobile layout
StatusPreview build

The brief

Mind Renewal Care is a therapy NGO. This is the opposite discipline to a fashion site: the visitor is not browsing for pleasure, they are looking for help and they are probably on a phone. The design has to lower the temperature, not perform.

So the site leads with answers to the questions someone actually has: what the service is, who delivers it, what a session involves, and one obvious next action.

What is on the site

  • Service cards for psychiatric care, counselling and community programmes, each with what it covers and what happens next.
  • A team and approach section that carries the credibility the audience needs before they make contact.
  • Impact reporting so the organisation's work is legible to funders as well as patients.
  • A single call to action: schedule a session, repeated where it is needed and nowhere it is not.

Mobile was the default, not the fallback

The mobile layout was designed as its own thing rather than a squeezed desktop: navigation that works one-handed, imagery that does not cost a data plan, and type sizes that stay readable on a cheap phone. That is the same discipline the desktop layout then inherits.
