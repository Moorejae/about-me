About

I design AI workflows, direct the build, and test them until they hold up.

I grew up handling customer problems face to face in my family's electronics shop, so I know where people get stuck. That shop is also where nine years of customer support started.

ForChi runs three business processes unattended. REZMO gives people portable AI memory across Claude and Grok, with setup guides and troubleshooting written for people who do not read code.

AI agents write my code. My part is the design, the specs, the review and the testing. I reproduce bugs, test APIs, log edge cases and write documentation users can follow without a call. I own what ships.

I read configs and logs and work with APIs, webhooks, MCP and OAuth 2.0, and I use SQL to investigate problems.

Open to internship, junior and mid-level roles in AI automation, AI operations, implementation and QA.

REZMO: Portable AI Memory

Outcome: solved AI context loss for everyday users across Claude and Grok, then backed it with setup guides and troubleshooting so people can get running without a support call.

A user's conversations and files live in their own Google Drive, and any connected model picks up the same context through an MCP server I built.

ForChi: Telegram Automation Agent

Outcome: automated three parallel business processes, content pipelines, social distribution and applicant screening, to remove repetitive manual work.

Three workflows run unattended: social posts to Facebook and LinkedIn, a YouTube video pipeline, and a job-application engine that scores roles with Gemini and applies through Lever and Ashby.

WCM: Live Pricing Model

Outcome: a football outcome model checked against market prices, with skipped cases logged and accuracy audited after every settled result.

The model starts from market prices, shrinks its own estimate toward them, and records the fixtures it skips in a checkpoint dataset so it can be calibrated rather than argued about.
