About

I bridge users and AI systems. Nine years solving complex user problems, paired with hands-on experience designing AI workflows, technical documentation and customer-ready systems.

Customer support taught me exactly where people get stuck. Working with AI agents lets me turn those stuck points into calm support, clear specs and automated workflows. ForChi runs three business processes unattended. REZMO gives people portable AI memory across Claude and Grok, with setup guides and troubleshooting written for people who do not read code.

I reproduce bugs, test APIs and write documentation users can follow without a call, without waiting on engineering. I direct the AI agents, review their output and own what ships. The goal is not to compete for a traditional coding seat. It is to make technical systems usable, reliable and calm for the people who depend on them.

The technical fluency underneath is real: APIs, webhooks, MCP, SQL, and cloud environments across Cloudflare, AWS, Google Cloud (including Vertex AI) and Oracle Cloud.

Open to AI Operations, Solutions and Implementation, Product Ops, and Technical Support roles.

REZMO: Portable AI Memory

Outcome: solved AI context loss for everyday users across Claude and Grok, then backed it with setup guides and troubleshooting so people can get running without a support call.

A user's conversations and files live in their own Google Drive, and any connected model picks up the same context through an MCP server I built.

ForChi: Telegram Automation Agent

Outcome: automated three parallel business processes, content pipelines, social distribution and applicant screening, to remove repetitive manual work.

Three workflows run unattended: social posts to Facebook and LinkedIn, a YouTube video pipeline, and a job-application engine that scores roles with Gemini and applies through Lever and Ashby.

Read the story

WCM: Live Pricing Model

Outcome: turned an ambiguous modelling problem into a strict, testable specification, with skipped edge cases logged and prediction accuracy audited after every settled result.

The model uses closing-line priors, a probability shrunk toward the market, and a checkpoint dataset that records the bets it skips so it can be calibrated rather than argued about.
