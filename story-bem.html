BEM Africa: a site that moves

A fashion house needed a lookbook, not a catalogue. Built end to end: visual system, scroll-driven homepage, admin console, deployment.

ClientBEM Africa
RoleDesign, build, deploy
StatusPreview build

The brief

BEM Africa is a fashion house. The obvious build is a product grid with a hero image, and it would have flattened the brand into a shop. The brief that actually worked: make the homepage behave like a lookbook, something you scroll through rather than browse.

The build

  • A scroll-driven homepage. Full-bleed editorial panels, staged reveals and motion transitions between looks, so the collection reads as a sequence rather than a list.
  • A real visual system. A type scale, spacing rhythm and colour set that survives real photography and real copy, not lorem ipsum.
  • Responsive throughout. The motion and the layouts were designed to collapse cleanly rather than being retrofitted for mobile.
  • An admin console. Products and content are editable by the client, so a hero change is not a developer ticket.
  • Deployment. Hosting, domain, TLS and the build pipeline, handled as part of the work rather than handed off as a zip file.
If the client cannot update it, it is not finished.

What it is

A preview build: the site and the admin are complete and working, but it is not connected to a public domain yet. The walkthrough below is the demo.

Public site and admin console walkthrough.

What Kester said

Kester, BEM Africa“Victor understood the brand before he wrote a line of code. The site feels like the collection, the motion, the pacing, all of it, and I can update it myself without calling anyone. That is exactly what I asked for and more than I expected.”

What I took from it

Motion is a tool, not decoration. Scroll animation earns its place only when it carries the reader forward; the moment it becomes a showreel it starts costing the client visitors. The other lesson is administrative: building the console changes the shape of the site, so it has to be designed at the same time, not bolted on afterwards.
